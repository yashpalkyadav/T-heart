The Challenge & Our Breakthrough Solution

Addressing structural inefficiencies in educational resource consumption[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span).

1. Problem Identification

Why do educational resources remain unused?

  • Students stop using textbooks/reference books after one academic year[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span).
  • Many usable books and stationery items sit idle at home[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span).
  • Buying new academic resources creates unnecessary financial burden[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span).
  • High-value resources get discarded instead of reused[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span).

2. Our Innovative Solution

Connecting peer-to-peer student needs[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span).

  • Sell: List usable materials with condition transparency[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span).
  • Exchange: Swap needed titles without financial drag[span_15](start_span)[span_15](end_span).
  • Donate: Direct transfer to underprivileged peers[span_16](start_span)[span_16](end_span).
  • USP: Dedicated circular ecosystem tailored exclusively for educational ecosystems[span_17](start_span)[span_17](end_span).
